# Rights and proposed release licensing

This is a private preparation draft. The following source-specific rights must be retained in any eventual release.

| Material | Rights record |
| --- | --- |
| `vendor/openai_math/lean/**` | Upstream Apache-2.0, reproduced at `vendor/openai_math/LICENSE` |
| Adapted Kneser source under `Erdos790/External/**` | Upstream Apache-2.0 and preserved author headers, reproduced at `licenses/Kneser-Apache-2.0.txt` |
| Imported JENW1N/lkruer lower-bound source | The pinned upstream tree has no LICENSE file. The owner reports authorization exists and will supply the grant or confirmation. Its scope must be recorded before public release |
| Newly authored upper-bound source, bridge and submission documentation | Apache-2.0 is the proposed release license, subject to contributor rights confirmation. The complete text is at `licenses/Apache-2.0.txt` |
| Lean, Mathlib and fetched packages | The packages' own licenses, at their pinned revisions |

No blanket license declaration overrides an upstream file's rights or missing grant. The private release checklist records the remaining confirmations. No signed grant, private identity document, contact address or payment information belongs in the public repository.
